Who we are

🧬

What is iGEM Okinawa?

We are a team of students from Okinawa tackling global challenges through synthetic biology, developing solutions rooted in our local environment.

🏫

3 Participating Institutions

Our team brings together diverse talents from National Institute of Technology, Okinawa College (KOSEN), Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST), and University of the Ryukyus.

🚀

Our Vision

To empower the next generation of scientists in Okinawa, bridge local wisdom with global innovation, and build a sustainable future using synthetic biology.

Current Project: 2026

AI x Biology: Engineering phages against gastrointestinal pathogens

"We are working on developing bacteriophages that are resilient to low-pH environments, such as the inside of the stomach. In this development, we utilize a proprietary AI platform to evaluate their acid tolerance.

Our Team

Students from OIST, NIT Okinawa College, and the University of the Ryukyus — united by synthetic biology.

Wet

Wet Lab

Responsible for all experimental work — phage isolation, protein engineering, cloning, and lab validation of AI-predicted variants.

Dry

Dry Lab

Builds and runs the AI-based pipeline for predicting low-pH resistant phage protein variants. Handles computational modeling and data analysis.

Human Practice

Human Practice

Connects our science to society — engaging with local farmers, medical professionals, and communities to ensure our work addresses real needs.

Funding

Funding

Manages sponsorship outreach, donation campaigns, and financial planning to sustain our research and international competition entry.

Wiki / Video
