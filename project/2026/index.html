Past Activities & Previous Teams

Our journey didn't start this year. Explore the legacy, past projects, and the alumni who laid the foundation for iGEM Okinawa.

2025

Mango & Thrips — Smart Pest Detection System

iGEM Okinawa 2025 tackled a real agricultural problem: distinguishing harmful C-type thrips from harmless YT-type thrips on Okinawan mango farms, developing an electrochemical detection device, a species-specific kit, and a synthetic biology-based countermeasure.

2024

Early outreach & education pilots

Ran introductory synthetic biology sessions with local students and shaped our long-term competition roadmap.
